What to read here

All four models clear 0.79 accuracy and 0.88 ROC-AUC, so the signal is strong. The choice of “best” depends on the goal:

  • Random Forest leads on ROC-AUC — best at ranking risk.
  • SVM leads on accuracy, recall and F1 — fewest missed patients.
  • Logistic Regression is fastest and most interpretable.
  • Gradient Boosting is a strong, balanced all-rounder.
